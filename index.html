Beginner's Guide to Basketball

Basic Rules

  • Players must bounce the ball continuously with one hand while moving with their feet.
  • Baskets are worth two points, or three points if shot from outside the arc.
  • The offensive team must attempt a shot that hits the rim or goes in within 24 seconds
  • Stepping out of bounds results in a turnover which gives it to the enemy team.
